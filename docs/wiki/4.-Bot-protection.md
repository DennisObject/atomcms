# Bot protection

Atom supports Cloudflare Turnstile and Google reCAPTCHA v2. Use one at a time. Turn the other off in the CMS settings.

## Cloudflare Turnstile

1. Create a widget for your hotel's hostname in the [Turnstile dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/).
2. Add the keys to `.env`. Keep the secret key private.

   ```dotenv
   TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY=your-site-key
   TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY=your-secret-key
   ```

3. In Housekeeping, under CMS settings, set `cloudflare_turnstile_enabled=1` and `google_recaptcha_enabled=0`.

## Google reCAPTCHA

1. Register your hostname in the [reCAPTCHA console](https://www.google.com/recaptcha/admin/). Choose **reCAPTCHA v2, "I'm not a robot" Checkbox**.
2. Add the keys to `.env`:

   ```dotenv
   GOOGLE_RECAPTCHA_SITE_KEY=your-site-key
   GOOGLE_RECAPTCHA_SECRET_KEY=your-secret-key
   ```

3. In CMS settings, set `google_recaptcha_enabled=1` and `cloudflare_turnstile_enabled=0`.

## Check that it works

1. Rebuild the config cache after you change `.env`.
2. Open login and registration in a browser where you are signed out.
3. A valid challenge must submit. A missing or invalid challenge must be rejected.

Your theme must load the provider's script. Turn off Cloudflare Rocket Loader for Atom.

In [headless mode](10.-Headless-mode.md), your frontend shows the challenge. Read the keys and the enabled provider from `GET /api/v1/bootstrap`.
